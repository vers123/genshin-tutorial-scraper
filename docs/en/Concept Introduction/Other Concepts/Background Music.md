# I. Functions of Background Music

Through Background Music Management, Creators (Craftspeople) can customize background music for their gameplay. During subsequent gameplay, it can also be edited via the Node Graph

In Co-Op Mode, different players can have different background music

# II. Background Music Editing

## 1. Entry Points

![](../../images/44cf5820fcc664a0.png)

Click the System Menu button (top-left) to open the menu interface

![](../../images/437c4bb2095e4e63.png)

Click [Manage Background Music] to enter the background music management interface

## 2. Detailed Introduction

![](../../images/75133a86f0caa3e8.png)

*Background Music Assets*: Allows selecting a specific background music asset from the Music Library

![](../../images/1651cf9c86625078.png)

*Effective Time Range*: Specifies the portion of the full music asset to be used as the actual background music playback

*Volume*: Configures the background music volume

*Playback Speed*: Default is 1. Adjusts the playback speed of the background music. Extremely fast or slow values may affect the listening experience

*Fade In/Out*: Determines whether fade-in and fade-out effects are applied at the start and end of playback

*Loop Playback*: Determines whether the background music restarts automatically after finishing

*Interval Time (s)*: If loop playback is enabled, waits for the specified interval after playback ends before playing again
