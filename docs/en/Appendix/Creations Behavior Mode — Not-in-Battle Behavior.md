# I. Not-in-Battle Behavior Archive

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Behavior Mode | Not-in-Battle Behavior | Not-in-Battle Behavior Description | Example Illustration |
| Normal | Standing | During Stage runtime, unfriendly units remain standing and do not move when not in battle |  |
| Normal | Wandering | During Stage runtime, when unfriendly units are not in battle, they wander by randomly selecting points within their *Territory*. |  |
| Normal | Sitting | During Stage runtime, unfriendly units remain seated and do not move when not in battle | ![](../images/207514f38a448d1e.png) |
| Normal | Sleeping | During Stage runtime, unfriendly units remain sleeping and do not move when not in battle | ![](../images/ecbbde8dd0ca63d1.png) |
| Normal | Dancing | During Stage runtime, unfriendly units remain dancing when not in battle | ![](../images/e015316c251dc046.png) |
| Normal | Hiding | During stage runtime, if the unfriendly unit is not in battle, it remains underground. | ![](../images/cb64c0de56090115.png) |
| Normal | Underground | During Stage runtime, unfriendly units remain underground when not in battle | ![](../images/b8a09a4ec04976a7.png) |
| Normal | Floating | During Stage runtime, unfriendly units remain airborne when not in battle | ![](../images/e8b513f3eb9c4b4f.png) |
